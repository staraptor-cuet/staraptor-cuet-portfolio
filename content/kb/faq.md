## Team

### What is Team Staraptor CUET?

Team Staraptor CUET is a student team at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), Bangladesh, that
designs and builds VTOL (vertical take-off and landing) unmanned aerial vehicles. The team has 20 crew members in
six sub-teams and its tagline is "Engineers Bound by Flight". The team describes itself as the first VTOL drone
design team in Bangladesh from CUET.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/ ; https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/crew

### When was Team Staraptor CUET founded?

The team website gives 2026 as the founding year of Team Staraptor CUET.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/

### How many members does Team Staraptor CUET have?

Team Staraptor CUET has 20 crew members. The crew is organised in six sub-teams: Design, Simulation, Branding &
Communication, Research, Software and Documentation, with a Project Lead on the Project Board.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/crew

### Which competitions is Team Staraptor CUET aiming for?

Team Staraptor CUET has two competition goals: the METU VTOL Aircraft Design Competition and TEKNOFEST, both in
Türkiye. The team has not yet published which TEKNOFEST category or which year it will enter, and it has not
published any competition results.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/vtol-2026 ; team statement, November 2026

## Aircraft

### What aircraft is Team Staraptor CUET building?

Team Staraptor CUET is building the Staraptor VTOL V1, a fixed-wing VTOL unmanned aerial vehicle. It takes off and
lands vertically and flies forward on its wing. It uses electric brushless motors, a Pixhawk flight controller and
GPS. The first prototype iteration has been completed.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/aircraft

### What are the specifications of the Staraptor VTOL V1?

The team website lists these design targets for the Staraptor VTOL V1, which are not flight-verified: wingspan
1.340 m, length 0.99 m, approximate weight 2 kg, payload capacity 8 kg, cruise speed 60-70 km/h, endurance 90 min,
wing loading 55-75 g/dm², airfoil Eppler E205.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/aircraft

### Has the Staraptor VTOL V1 flown yet?

The team website lists four stages: Conceptual Design (completed), Simulation (completed), Prototype (in progress)
and Successful Launch (not yet reached). No first-flight date or flight-test data has been published as of
13 November 2026.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/aircraft ; https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/progress

## Competitions

### What is the METU VTOL Aircraft Design Competition?

The METU VTOL Aircraft Design Competition is held at Middle East Technical University (METU) in Türkiye. University
teams design, build and autonomously fly VTOL aircraft. Under the 2025 rules the aircraft must weigh under 20 kg
including payload, use electric propulsion only, and fly three missions: Ferry Flight, Pirouette and Fixed-Wing
Loiter Performance. Check the official rules page before relying on any rule.

Source: https://vtol-ae.metu.edu.tr/en/rules.html

### When is the next METU VTOL competition?

The 9th METU VTOL Aircraft Design Competition was held on 18-19 October 2025. No announcement of a 10th edition was
found on the official competition site as of 13 November 2026. Check the official site for updates.

Source: https://vtol-ae.metu.edu.tr/en

### What is TEKNOFEST?

TEKNOFEST is an aerospace and technology festival in Türkiye. The Turkish Technology Team Foundation (T3 Vakfı)
lists TEKNOFEST among its brands and describes it as organised in partnership with numerous institutions.
TEKNOFEST includes technology competitions, among them the International UAV Competition, organised by TÜBİTAK,
with the categories Fixed Wing, Rotary Wing and Free Mission.

Source: https://teknofest.org/en ; https://t3vakfi.org/en ; https://teknofest.org/en/competitions/competition/45

### Which TEKNOFEST category can a VTOL aircraft enter?

Under the 2026 specification of the TEKNOFEST International UAV Competition, VTOL aircraft can only compete in the
Free Mission category; they cannot enter the Fixed Wing or Rotary Wing categories. Team Staraptor CUET has not yet
published which category or year it will enter. Check the official specification before relying on this rule.

Source: https://teknofest.org/en/competitions/competition/45
