## Current sponsors of Team Staraptor CUET

Team Staraptor CUET lists two partners on its Sponsors page. The Title Partner is Epic Properties Ltd. The Gold
Partner is Pusti Group. The Platinum Partner and Media Partner positions are open and marked "TBA" (to be
announced).

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/sponsors

## Sponsorship tiers of Team Staraptor CUET

Team Staraptor CUET shows four partner tiers on its website: Title Partner, Platinum Partners, Gold Partners and
Media Partners. The website does not publish prices or benefit lists for the tiers. For tier details, see the
team's sponsorship proposal, which can be downloaded from the Sponsors page, or ask the team by email. The
chatbot cannot quote prices or promise benefits; the team confirms these directly.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/sponsors

## How to become a sponsor of Team Staraptor CUET

Organisations that want to sponsor Team Staraptor CUET can email the team at staraptorcuet@gmail.com. The
sponsorship proposal is available through the "Download Proposal" button on the Sponsors page.
The team states that the development of its VTOL UAV depends on the support of its partners and that it is
actively looking for organisations to join its mission.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/sponsors
