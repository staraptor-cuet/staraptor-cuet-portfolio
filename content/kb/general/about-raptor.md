## What Raptor is

Raptor is the AI assistant on the Team Staraptor CUET website. Raptor answers questions about the team, the crew,
the Staraptor VTOL V1 aircraft, the METU VTOL competition, TEKNOFEST, sponsorship, joining the team, and drone and
VTOL engineering in general. Raptor is a computer program, not a person and not a member of the team. Raptor can
be opened from the "Ask Raptor" button on every page or on the full page at /ask.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/ask

## How Raptor produces an answer

Raptor searches a knowledge base written by the team. The knowledge base contains the team's website content and
facts taken from official pages, such as the METU VTOL rules page, the TEKNOFEST specification and the ArduPilot,
PX4, NASA and FAA documentation. A large language model then writes the answer using only the passages that were
found, and adds numbered source links. Raptor does not browse the internet and does not know anything that is not
in the knowledge base. Each knowledge file carries the date it was last verified.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/ask

## What Raptor cannot do

Raptor cannot speak for CUET, promise sponsorship benefits or team positions, accept applications, take messages
for members, send email, book meetings, or give personal contact details of members. Raptor does not give
competition results, dates or rules that are not in the knowledge base. Raptor does not help with homework, coding
tasks or topics outside the team and drone engineering. Raptor always replies in English, also when the question
is written in Bangla, Banglish or another language. Raptor can be wrong; for anything important, check the linked
source or email the team at staraptorcuet@gmail.com.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/ask

## Privacy of conversations with Raptor

Questions and answers are stored for 30 days so the team can improve the assistant. They are stored with an
anonymous visitor code that changes every day, not with a name or an IP address. Email addresses and phone numbers
typed into the chat are removed before storage. The conversation shown on screen is kept in the visitor's own
browser and can be deleted with the bin icon. The question text is processed by Groq, the company that runs the
language model. Visitors should not type personal or confidential information into the chat.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/ask

## When Raptor shows "FAQ mode" or says it is at its usage limit

Raptor runs on a free usage allowance of the language-model provider. When many people ask questions at the same
time, or the daily allowance is used up, Raptor switches to FAQ mode and shows the closest passage from the
knowledge base instead of a written answer. Normal answers return automatically, usually within a minute and at
the latest the next day. The team email staraptorcuet@gmail.com always works.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/ask

## Reporting a wrong answer from Raptor

A visitor who sees a wrong or unhelpful answer can press the thumbs-down button under that answer. The team
reviews these reports and corrects the knowledge base. Corrections and missing information can also be sent to
staraptorcuet@gmail.com.
