## What CUET is

CUET stands for Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology. The official CUET history text says CUET is the former Bangladesh Institute of Technology, Chittagong (BIT Chittagong). It describes CUET as an autonomous institute that awards its own degrees in engineering education in Bangladesh. In 2003 the institute became a public university. The President of Bangladesh is the Chancellor of the university. Team Staraptor CUET is a student team at CUET.

The official website gives this postal address and contact details for CUET:

- Pahartoli, Raozan, Chattogram - 4349, Bangladesh
- Email: registrar@cuet.ac.bd
- Phone: +880-31-714946

The postal address uses the spelling "Chattogram", while the university's name uses the older spelling "Chittagong". Both names refer to the same city.

Source: https://www.cuet.ac.bd/about/history ; https://www.cuet.ac.bd/api/about/about.json ; https://www.cuet.ac.bd

## History and former names of CUET

The official CUET history text and the "CUET Overview" table describe how the university developed in three stages:

| Date | Name and status (as the official site states) |
|---|---|
| 1968 | Engineering College, Chittagong, was established. It worked as the Faculty of Engineering of the University of Chittagong. |
| 1 July 1986 | A Government ordinance converted the college into an Institute of Technology: Bangladesh Institute of Technology (BIT), Chittagong. |
| 1 September 2003 | The institute was converted into a public university, now Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET). |

The official overview table also lists these figures for CUET: campus area 171 acres, 5 faculties, 3 institutes, 2 research centres and 5 halls (student residences). It lists the higher degrees offered as M.Sc. Engg., M.Phil. and Ph.D. The same table gives about 4,500 students and more than 200 faculty members. These figures are as stated on the official site; the site's own pages do not all give the same department count, so this file uses the faculty listing below for departments.

Source: https://www.cuet.ac.bd/about/history ; https://www.cuet.ac.bd/api/about/about.json

## Where CUET is located

The official CUET overview says the university is beside the Chittagong Kaptai road (the overview table gives the location as "Kaptai Road Chittagong"), about 25 kilometres from the city of Chittagong. The overview describes Chittagong as the main sea port and the second largest city of Bangladesh. It says the campus covers about 171 acres of hills and plain land.

The official site has a page called "CUET Official Logo and Directions" and a campus map page. Visitors who need travel directions should use those official pages or contact the Registrar's office (registrar@cuet.ac.bd). This knowledge base does not give travel directions.

## How undergraduate admission to CUET works

The official CUET admission page says CUET offers undergraduate programmes in:

- Engineering
- Urban and Regional Planning (BURP)
- Architecture (B.Arch.)

The page describes the admission steps for students from Bangladesh:

- Candidates sit an admission test.
- A candidate's examination centre depends on the marks from the Higher Secondary (HSC) or equivalent examination in four subjects (Mathematics, Science, Chemistry and English) and on the candidate's preference.
- Admission depends on the merit rank the candidate obtains in the admission test.
- For candidates who qualify in the test, the application and the selection order of university and department are done online.

On 2026-11-13 the page said no admission test was scheduled at present. It said admission notices, eligibility rules, application procedures and test schedules will be published only through CUET's official website and other authorised channels.

Source: https://www.cuet.ac.bd/admission

## Admission for foreign students and postgraduates at CUET

The official CUET admission page has a section on admission of foreign students to the undergraduate programmes. The page states these points:

- CUET's International Affairs Office handles admission for foreign students directly.
- Applicants must submit academic transcripts, a passport copy, and relevant certificates attested by their Ministry of Education or Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
- Admission is based on earlier results in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and English, along with overall academic merit.
- Selected applicants receive an official offer letter. The letter includes instructions on visa processing, enrolment and accommodation.
- Applications go through CUET's foreign admission portal or by official email.

The same page has an "Apply Here" link for postgraduate studies, which leads to https://webs.cuet.ac.bd/. The admission page itself does not describe the postgraduate programmes. Deadlines and fees were not shown on the page when it was checked.

## Who regulates drones in Bangladesh

The Civil Aviation Authority of Bangladesh (CAAB) regulates civil aviation in Bangladesh. Under its Regulator menu (the menu is in Bangla), the CAAB website has a section called UAS/Drone. UAS means unmanned aircraft system. RPAS means remotely piloted aircraft system. Both terms describe a drone together with its ground equipment. On 2026-11-13, that section listed three documents:

| Document (English name) | Link |
|---|---|
| ANO on UAS/Drone (DRAFT) | http://new.caab.gov.bd/corporate/drone.pdf |
| Circular for UAV/Drone | http://new.caab.gov.bd/corporate/rpas.pdf |
| Flight application form | http://new.caab.gov.bd/corporate/rpasapp.pdf |

ANO stands for Air Navigation Order. The CAAB site marks the ANO on UAS/Drone as a draft. CAAB also publishes an application form for drone flights. The PDFs are scanned images, and their text could not be read during verification. This file therefore does not state registration classes, weight categories, height limits or restricted zones.

Anyone planning to fly a drone in Bangladesh must check CAAB's website or contact CAAB for the current rules before flying.

Source: https://www.caab.gov.bd ; http://new.caab.gov.bd/corporate/rpas.pdf ; http://new.caab.gov.bd/corporate/drone.pdf

## Visa basics for Bangladeshi citizens travelling to Türkiye

The Republic of Türkiye Ministry of Foreign Affairs publishes visa information by nationality. For Bangladesh, the page says ordinary passport holders must have a visa. An ordinary passport is the standard passport most citizens hold.

The same page describes a conditional e-Visa, which is an online visa:

- Bangladeshi ordinary passport holders who also hold a valid visa or residence permit from a Schengen country, the USA, the UK or Ireland may get an e-Visa through www.evisa.gov.tr, if they meet certain conditions.
- This e-Visa is single-entry and valid for one month.
- Bangladeshi holders of diplomatic and official/service passports are exempt from visas for travels of up to 90 days.
- The page says the e-Visa is valid only when the purpose of travel is tourism or commerce. For other purposes, such as work and study, visas are given by Turkish embassies or consulates.

This file gives no visa advice. Travellers should follow the official Turkish pages and the Turkish embassy in Dhaka.

Source: https://www.mfa.gov.tr/visa-information-for-foreigners.en.mfa ; https://www.evisa.gov.tr/en/
