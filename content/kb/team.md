## Who Team Staraptor CUET is

Team Staraptor CUET is a student team that designs and builds VTOL (vertical take-off and landing) unmanned aerial
vehicles. The team is based at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET) in Chattogram, Bangladesh.
The team's tagline is "Engineers Bound by Flight". The team website describes Team Staraptor CUET as the first VTOL
drone design team in Bangladesh from CUET; this is the team's own description. The website gives 2026 as the
founding year. The Crew page lists 24 student team members, 4 advisors and 3 instructors (31 people in total);
the home page statistic still shows a crew size of 20, the size before four members joined in November 2026.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/ ; https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/crew

## Vision of Team Staraptor CUET

The stated vision of Team Staraptor CUET is to become a leading UAV team that advances drone innovation through
engineering excellence and global competition. The team aims to contribute to Bangladesh's technological progress
by developing high-performance UAV systems and by inspiring the next generation of engineers.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/crew

## Mission of Team Staraptor CUET

The stated mission of Team Staraptor CUET is to design, build and operate drones that meet international standards,
to represent CUET on global platforms, and to address real-world challenges through practical UAV applications. The
team commits to continuous learning, teamwork and mentorship, and wants to build a lasting culture of innovation
that contributes to national development.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/crew

## Structure and sub-teams of Team Staraptor CUET

Team Staraptor CUET has 24 student team members, guided by 4 advisors and 3 instructors. The Project Board is led
by the Project Lead. The crew is organised in six
sub-teams:

- Design team: aircraft design and CAD.
- Simulation team: aerodynamic and structural simulation, including CFD.
- Branding & Communication team: branding, media and sponsor communication.
- Research team: technical research.
- Software team: flight software and autonomy.
- Documentation team: reports and documentation.

The website states 8+ technical roles and 4+ operational roles. The student members carry the batch codes
ME-22 and ME-23 (Mechanical Engineering) and CSE-23 (Computer Science and Engineering). The sub-team descriptions above are inferred from the sub-team names; the website does not publish
detailed role descriptions.

## Competition goals of Team Staraptor CUET

Team Staraptor CUET has two current competition goals: the METU VTOL Aircraft Design Competition (Ankara, Türkiye)
and TEKNOFEST (Türkiye). The team website presents the METU VTOL competition as the current objective. The team
has not yet published which TEKNOFEST competition category or which year it will enter. The team has not published
any competition results. For confirmed plans, contact the team at staraptorcuet@gmail.com.

Source: https://staraptorcuet.vercel.app/progress ; team statement, November 2026

## What Team Staraptor CUET is working on

Team Staraptor CUET is developing a hybrid tilt-rotor VTOL UAV named Staraptor VTOL V1 for the METU VTOL
Aircraft Design Competition 2026. The Our Progress page shows its status as "Active Prototype & Flight Ready" and reports
completed initial CFD simulations and prototype testing (entry dated 1 March 2026), with payload distribution and
motor thrust being validated. The website also has an Achievements page for flight-test milestones and competition
updates, which has no posts yet, and a Gallery with renders, workshop photos and CFD images.
